# Flight Helmet sample asset

Source: [Khronos glTF Sample Assets — Flight Helmet](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/4995a638c96dbc94376d3d6164e70fee3ac7f7a2/Models/FlightHelmet).

Revision: `4995a638c96dbc94376d3d6164e70fee3ac7f7a2`.

The upstream model's legal notice identifies it as public domain, 2018, under
[Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (CC0-1.0)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode).
Gary Hsu is credited for conversion from Maya. Original notices are retained
in the downloaded `README.md`; the pinned file hashes are in `sample-assets.json`.

The model and textures are separate from this project's GPL code. The prepared
model in the application bundle is derived from this CC0 asset: node transforms
are flattened, the model is centered/scaled, and images are decoded/resized to
RGBA. No original source asset files are overwritten. Screenshots show that
prepared model rendered through Darling Metal.
